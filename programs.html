Two courses, Drishti certified

Skills That Lead to Real Income

Both courses run for 6 months, are free for enrolled students, and end with a certificate co-issued with our partner Drishti.

6Months
Livelihood Skill

Tailoring Course for Rural Women

A hands-on, 6-month course that takes rural women from the basics of hand and machine stitching through to garment construction and finishing — building the skills needed to stitch for their own family, take up tailoring work, or start a small business.

Download Brochure

Hand-stitching basics, using the sewing machine, thread and fabric handling.

Taking measurements, drafting basic patterns, and fabric cutting techniques.

Stitching blouses, salwar suits, kurtas and everyday garments end-to-end.

Finishing techniques, embellishment basics, and final practical assessment for certification.

6Months
Digital Skill

Diploma in Computer Application (DCA)

A structured, 8-module, 6-month diploma that takes students from computer basics to job-ready office and accounting skills, including Tally with GST.

Download Brochure

Introduction to computer hardware, software and fundamental concepts.

Navigating and managing the Windows operating system and its core tools.

Touch-typing practice for speed and accuracy in English and Hindi typing.

Word, Excel and PowerPoint for everyday documents, spreadsheets and presentations.

Safe and effective use of the internet, browsers, and professional email communication.

Fundamentals of networking concepts relevant to everyday office IT setups.

Accounting fundamentals and billing using Tally, including GST-compliant entries.

File and folder management, followed by hands-on practical assignments across all modules for final certification.
