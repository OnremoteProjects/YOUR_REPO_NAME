Who we are

It Takes a Village to Build a Livelihood

Ram Pyari Kundan Lal Mahajan NGO works with rural women and unemployed youth to build practical, job-ready skills. Through hands-on tailoring and computer application courses, our students gain not just a certificate, but the confidence and income to support their families.
