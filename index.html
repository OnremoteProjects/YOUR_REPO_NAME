A life of courage and service

In Revered Memory of Late Shri Kundanlal Mahajan

Late Shri Kundanlal Mahajan of Ali Beg, District Mirpur (POK), is remembered for his extraordinary courage and selfless sacrifice during the tribal invasion of Jammu & Kashmir in 1947.

He bravely resisted the invaders and attained martyrdom while attempting to rescue a young girl from abduction, sacrificing his life to protect her honor and dignity.

In his cherished memory, his family established the Rampyari Kundanlal Mahajan Memorial Foundation, dedicated to serving society through education and skill development.

The Foundation’s Skill Development Center at Shukartal, Muzaffarnagar provides completely free training in Computer Courses and Sewing & Tailoring, empowering youth, women, and economically weaker sections.

His courage lives on through every life empowered and every skill learned.
